# Problem Definition — AutoSenseAI

## 1. Background

Fleet-based organizations (logistics companies, rental services, corporate vehicle fleets) rely heavily on vehicle uptime to operate efficiently. In traditional fleet management systems, maintenance is largely reactive—issues are addressed only after a breakdown or warning light appears. Vehicle telemetry data exists but is often underutilized, siloed, or difficult to interpret in real time.

This results in unexpected failures, increased operational costs, inefficient maintenance scheduling, and reduced vehicle lifespan.

With the rise of connected vehicles and IoT sensors, large volumes of real-time vehicle data are now available. However, most fleet operators lack intelligent systems that convert this raw data into actionable insights.

---

## 2. Target Users

- **Fleet Managers** – responsible for vehicle availability and operational efficiency  
- **Maintenance Teams / Mechanics** – perform inspections and repairs  
- **Operations Managers** – oversee logistics performance and cost optimization

---

## 3. Core Problems

Current fleet management approaches suffer from:

### Reactive maintenance

- Repairs occur after failures instead of before

### Poor visibility into vehicle health

- No unified real-time overview of fleet condition

### Manual and inefficient decision making

- Maintenance scheduling based on fixed intervals rather than actual vehicle condition

### Underutilized telemetry data

- Sensor data is collected but not transformed into meaningful insights

### Lack of intelligent recommendations

- Operators must interpret complex technical data themselves

### Operational inefficiencies

- Increased downtime, higher repair costs, and reduced vehicle utilization

---

## 4. Proposed Solution

AutoSenseAI is an AI-powered SaaS platform that provides predictive maintenance and fleet intelligence by combining real-time telemetry ingestion, analytics, and LLM-driven insights.

The platform aims to:

- Continuously ingest vehicle sensor data  
- Detect anomalies and predict potential failures  
- Generate maintenance recommendations before breakdowns occur  
- Provide real-time dashboards for fleet health monitoring  
- Enable natural-language interaction through an AI assistant for operational insights

### Example Capabilities

- Identify vehicles at risk of failure within the next 24–72 hours  
- Recommend prioritized maintenance actions  
- Summarize fleet health in plain language  
- Answer questions like:
  - *“Which vehicles need urgent attention?”*  
  - *“What maintenance should be scheduled this week?”*

---

## 5. Success Metrics

The system will be considered successful if it can:

- Reduce unexpected vehicle breakdowns  
- Improve fleet uptime  
- Enable proactive maintenance scheduling  
- Lower maintenance costs  
- Provide clear, actionable AI-generated insights from raw telemetry data
